CryptPad is an open-source office suite for online collaboration. The server cannot read your documents, all encryption happens on your device.

Included applications:

- Rich Text documents
- Spreadsheets
- Code/Markdown editor
- Kanban boards
- Slides
- Whiteboard
- Polls

Features:

- Zero-knowledge encryption
- Real-time collaboration with author colors
- Chat and comment systems built-in
- Self-hosting available
- Official instance hosted by XWiki-SAS
