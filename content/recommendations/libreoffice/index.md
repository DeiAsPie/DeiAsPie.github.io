LibreOffice is a free, open-source office suite by The Document Foundation. It comes pre-installed on most Linux distributions.

Features:

- Writer (documents), Calc (spreadsheets), Impress (presentations), Draw, Base, Math
- Compatible with Microsoft Office formats
- Runs completely offline, with no tracking or data collection
- Windows, macOS, Linux
- Active development and regular updates

No subscription, no telemetry. Privacy-respecting alternative to Microsoft Office.
