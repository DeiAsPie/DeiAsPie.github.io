Bitwarden syncs passwords and logins across devices with zero-knowledge encryption, even Bitwarden can't read your data.

Features:

- Unlimited passwords and basic features on free tier
- Syncs across all devices
- Open-source and regularly audited
- 2FA/TOTP support
- Secure sharing with family/team members
- Self-hosting available (Vaultwarden)
